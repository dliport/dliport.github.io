Privacy by design

Your audio stays close.

Speech analysis, fluency reconstruction, and voice transformation happen on your device. No account is needed for speech analysis.

You control exports and optional contributions. If you contribute a recording to improve SPEFA, the app prepares voice-transformed audio and analysis for you to review and send. Spoken words are preserved, and voice transformation does not guarantee anonymity.

How SPEFA handles your information →

A tool for speech awareness

SPEFA provides automated results that can be inaccurate. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent a medical condition. For clinical concerns, consult a qualified speech-language professional.
