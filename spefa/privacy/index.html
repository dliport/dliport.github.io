SPEFA: Speech Fluency — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

SPEFA: Speech Fluency (“SPEFA,” “we,” or “the app”) is designed to analyze speech privately on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, when information may leave your device, and how to contact us.

1. On-device processing

Audio recording, speech analysis, stuttering detection, fluency reconstruction, and voice de-identification are performed on your device. SPEFA has no user account, advertising SDK, analytics service, or automatic server connection. Your recordings and analysis remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share or contribute them.

2. Optional contributions

SPEFA may allow you to voluntarily contribute data to help improve the app’s speech-analysis model. The app processes the recording on your device before contribution and prepares a de-identified audio file and related analysis information. The spoken words are preserved, while the processing is intended to remove the primary voice biometric characteristics. De-identification is not a guarantee of complete anonymity.

Nothing is sent automatically. You review the contribution in your own mail or sharing interface and must choose to send it. If you send a contribution by email, we receive the contributed audio and analysis information, as well as the email address associated with the message.

3. How contributed information is used

We use voluntarily contributed audio and analysis information only to improve SPEFA’s speech-analysis model and related app functionality. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or tracking, or intentionally share it with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain contributed information only for as long as reasonably necessary for model improvement and related support. You may request deletion of a contribution at any time by emailing newlifecreation01@gmail.com from the address used to send it. We will use reasonable efforts to locate and delete the contribution and confirm the request.

5. Exports and sharing

When you use SPEFA’s export or sharing features, files are sent only to the destination you select through Apple’s system share or mail interface. SPEFA does not receive those files unless you explicitly send them to us.

6. Microphone access

SPEFA requests microphone access so you can record speech for analysis. Microphone access is controlled by iOS and can be revoked in Settings at any time.

7. Purchases

If you purchase an optional feature through the App Store, Apple processes the transaction. SPEFA does not receive your payment card number or other payment credentials. Apple’s own terms and privacy policy apply to App Store transactions.

8. Children

SPEFA is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Not a medical device

SPEFA is an automated speech-screening and awareness aid. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition and is not a substitute for professional medical or speech advice.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the current version took effect.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contribution deletion requests, or other concerns, contact us at newlifecreation01@gmail.com.
