Help with SPEFA

Get started, manage your recordings, and reach us with questions.

Contact support

Email newlifecreation01@gmail.com. For a technical issue, describe what happened and include your device model, iOS version, and the app version if available. Avoid sending sensitive recordings or personal details unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Record and analyze speech

  1. Allow microphone access when prompted. You can change this later in iOS Settings.
  2. Start a recording, speak, and stop when finished. You can also import a supported audio file.
  3. Review the highlighted segments and compare original and reconstructed playback. Automated results may be inaccurate.

Where does my audio go?

Speech processing runs on your device. Audio leaves your device only when you choose to export, share, or contribute it. See the privacy policy for the full explanation.

Optional contributions

The contribution flow prepares voice-transformed audio and related analysis, asks for consent, and opens the iOS Mail interface. You decide whether to send it. Spoken words remain, and the transformation cannot guarantee anonymity. Never contribute recordings containing private details about you or another person.

Delete a contribution

Email newlifecreation01@gmail.com from the address you used to send the contribution and identify the message so it can be located. Our retention and deletion terms are described in the privacy policy.

Optional upgrades and purchases

If an optional upgrade is available, its current price is shown in the app before purchase. Apple handles App Store payments. Use Restore purchases in Settings when restoring a previous purchase on the same Apple Account.

Speech or clinical concerns

SPEFA is a speech-awareness aid and is not a medical device. Consult a qualified speech-language professional for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical advice.
